# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome people of every background and
level of experience, and criticise ideas and code, never people. Harassment, insults, and personal or
political attacks are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else the project is
discussed.

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub rather than in a
public thread. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or
contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who break it repeatedly or seriously, as
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
